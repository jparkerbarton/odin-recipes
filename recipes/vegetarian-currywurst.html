Vegetarian Currywurst Recipe

By Ziko van Dijk - Own work, CC BY-SA 4.0, Link

Description:

A piquant take on an old sausage classic! Begin with some sauteed veggies, and add some turmeric to sausage for that extra zest. Add some french fries for that authentic feel.

Ingredients

  • Vegetarian sausage substitute (2-4)
  • White Onion, sliced
  • Green Bell Pepper, sliced
  • Neutral Oil (2 tbsp)
  • Black pepper, salt, cayenne pepper, turmeric powder

Steps

  1. Add oil to warm skillet.
  2. Add onion and bell pepper. Cook for about 10 minutes or until browning.
  3. Add meat substitute sausage and cook until browned.
  4. Add spice mixture to taste.
  5. Serve optionally with pretzel bun, catsup, mayonnaise, and french fries.
